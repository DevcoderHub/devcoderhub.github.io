About This Website

This website provides educational information about the YouTube Partner Program (YPP), monetization requirements, Creator Awards, and common issues faced by creators. The goal is to help creators understand YouTube systems clearly without misinformation.

YouTube Partner Program Eligibility

  • Minimum 1,000 subscribers
  • 4,000 valid public watch hours (or Shorts eligibility)
  • No active Community Guidelines strikes
  • Original and policy-compliant content
  • 2-step verification enabled

YouTube Creator Awards

Creator Awards (Silver, Gold, Diamond Play Buttons) are given after YouTube reviews a channel’s eligibility. Awards are not automatic and may be delayed if content quality, reuse, or policy compliance is under review.
